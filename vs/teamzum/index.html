Touchline Tales or Teamzum? What each one is for

Teamzum is a free platform for running a whole grassroots club. Here's a fair look at what it does well, what a Touchline Tales team website does differently, and how they can sit side by side.

By Murph Hartley, Touchline Tales · 26 October 2026 · 4 minute read

What Teamzum does well

  • The whole club in one place, for free. Teamzum covers registrations, player records, fixtures, training and messages to parents, with no monthly fee for the club platform.
  • A free club website. News, fixtures and results, team pages and sponsors, run from the same place as everything else.
  • Money and kit. Direct Debit subs, card payments and a club shop. Teamzum earns from fees on payments rather than charging the club.

If your committee wants one free system to run registrations, subs and a club website, Teamzum is built for exactly that.

Where a team website is different

  • A season you can share and keep. Every result, scorer and match report sits on one link that stays up. A grandparent can look back at October in March without scrolling a chat.
  • No app for parents to download. It's a website that works on any phone. Parents can save it to their home screen, and that's also how goal alerts reach iPhones.
  • Matchday as it happens. The coach taps goals, assists and subs from the touchline. Parents get the line-up, kick-off, every goal, half time and full time on their phone, with the scorer's first name.
  • Every child's season, counted. Games, goals, assists and milestones like a first goal are kept for each player, ending in a sticker book you can print.
  • Children first. First names only, team sites kept out of Google, and only the team's coaches can change anything. Your welfare officer decides whether scores are shown for Under 7s to Under 11s.
  • Built for your sport. Football from 5v5 to 11v11, and rugby union and rugby league with tries, conversions and kicks scored the right way.

Can a club use both?

Yes. Teamzum runs the club: registrations, payments, the shop and the club website. A Touchline Tales site is for one team's matchday and season: the live score, alerts to parents, every child's stats and the sticker book. It wears your club's crest and colours, and can link back to the club website.

Questions

Is Touchline Tales a club management system like Teamzum?

No. Touchline Tales doesn't do registrations, membership, subs or a club shop. It's one website per team, for matchday and the season record.

Does it replace our club website?

No, it sits alongside it. Each Touchline Tales site is for one team, in your club's crest, colours and sponsors.

How much does Touchline Tales cost?

£60 per team per season, July to June, plus £35 once for setup. Our first five founding teams pay nothing until June 2027.

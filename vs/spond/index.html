Touchline Tales or Spond? What each one is for

Spond is the app a lot of grassroots clubs already use. Here's an honest look at what it does well, what a Touchline Tales team website does differently, and how the two fit together.

By Murph Hartley, Touchline Tales · 26 October 2026 · 4 minute read

What Spond does well

  • It's free and it's everywhere. Spond doesn't charge teams to use it, and with well over a million users in the UK, there's a good chance your parents already have it.
  • Organising the week. Events, training, who's available and messages to parents all sit in one app.
  • Collecting money. Subs, match fees and kit payments can be taken in the app. Spond earns from a fee on those payments rather than charging the team.

If what you need is a way to organise the team and collect subs, Spond does that job well and costs the team nothing.

Where a team website is different

  • A season you can share and keep. Every result, scorer and match report sits on one link that stays up. A grandparent can look back at October in March without scrolling a chat.
  • No app for parents to download. It's a website that works on any phone. Parents can save it to their home screen, and that's also how goal alerts reach iPhones.
  • Matchday as it happens. The coach taps goals, assists and subs from the touchline. Parents get the line-up, kick-off, every goal, half time and full time on their phone, with the scorer's first name.
  • Every child's season, counted. Games, goals, assists and milestones like a first goal are kept for each player, ending in a sticker book you can print.
  • Children first. First names only, team sites kept out of Google, and only the team's coaches can change anything. Your welfare officer decides whether scores are shown for Under 7s to Under 11s.
  • Built for your sport. Football from 5v5 to 11v11, and rugby union and rugby league with tries, conversions and kicks scored the right way.

Can we use both?

Yes, and that's how we'd suggest it. Keep Spond for availability, messages and subs. Use Touchline Tales for the match itself and the season record: the live score, the alerts, every child's stats and the sticker book at the end. Touchline Tales doesn't collect subs or payments, so there's no overlap to untangle.

Questions

Is Touchline Tales a replacement for Spond?

Not really. Spond is for organising the team and collecting money. Touchline Tales is a website for matchday and the season record. Lots of teams will want both.

Do parents need another app?

No. Touchline Tales is a website that works on any phone, so there's nothing extra to download. Parents can save it to their home screen so it opens like an app.

Does Touchline Tales take subs or payments?

No. Touchline Tales doesn't handle subs or payments, so keep using whatever your club uses now for that.

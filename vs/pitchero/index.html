Touchline Tales or Pitchero? What each one is for

Pitchero has run club websites for grassroots sport for years. Here's an honest look at what it does well, what a Touchline Tales team website does differently, and when you might want both.

By Murph Hartley, Touchline Tales · 26 October 2026 · 4 minute read

What Pitchero does well

  • A proper club website and app. A branded site for the whole club, with news, fixtures, results and match reports for every team, plus a companion app.
  • Club-wide tools. Online registration, membership, team management, fundraising and the club lottery, with payments on its paid plans.
  • A free start. Its free plan suits a single-team club starting out. It carries third-party adverts, and paid plans add payments, a custom domain and fewer or no adverts.

If your club wants one public website for every team, built to be found in search, with membership and fundraising tied in, Pitchero is built for that.

Where a team website is different

  • A season you can share and keep. Every result, scorer and match report sits on one link that stays up. A grandparent can look back at October in March without scrolling a chat.
  • No app for parents to download. It's a website that works on any phone. Parents can save it to their home screen, and that's also how goal alerts reach iPhones.
  • Matchday as it happens. The coach taps goals, assists and subs from the touchline. Parents get the line-up, kick-off, every goal, half time and full time on their phone, with the scorer's first name.
  • Every child's season, counted. Games, goals, assists and milestones like a first goal are kept for each player, ending in a sticker book you can print.
  • Children first. First names only, team sites kept out of Google, and only the team's coaches can change anything. Your welfare officer decides whether scores are shown for Under 7s to Under 11s.
  • Built for your sport. Football from 5v5 to 11v11, and rugby union and rugby league with tries, conversions and kicks scored the right way.

Can a club use both?

Yes. Pitchero is the club's public front door. A Touchline Tales site is one team's matchday and season record, shared with that team's families: live alerts, every child's stats and the sticker book. Because team sites are kept out of Google and use first names only, they suit the children's side of things, while the club site does the public side.

Questions

Is Touchline Tales a club website like Pitchero?

No. Touchline Tales is one website per team, not one for the whole club. It doesn't do membership, registrations, payments or lotteries.

Are there adverts on Touchline Tales?

No. There are no adverts on any plan. Your club's own sponsors can be shown on the site.

Why are team sites kept out of Google?

Because they hold children's first names and match details. Parents get the link from the club, and search engines are told not to list the site.

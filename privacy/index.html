Privacy at Touchline Tales

What we collect when you get in touch, why, and how long we keep it.

Last updated October 2026

Touchline Tales makes team websites for grassroots football. This page covers the details you send us. If you're a parent looking at a team's site, that site has its own privacy page (the "Privacy" link at the bottom of it), because the club is in charge of its team's information.

What we collect

  • When you use the sign-up form: your name, email, phone number (if you give it), club, team, role, age group, format, how you heard about us, your message, and which link brought you here.
  • When you leave your details to try the demo site, or in the demo itself: your name, email, club and team, your role (if you give it), and which link brought you here. Nothing you do inside the demo is sent to us or saved anywhere; it stays in your browser.
  • When you email us: whatever you send.
  • When you visit these pages: anonymous visit counts. We see which pages were viewed, the site or link that brought people here, and the browser, device type, screen size and country, but never who you are. See "Visitor counts" below.
  • When a club signs up: the contact details we need to set up its site and send invoices.

Why we use it

To reply to you, follow up after you've tried the demo and, if your club signs up, to set up and run its site and keep our accounts. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and running the business, and the contract once a club signs up.

Who sees it

Only us. The form is delivered to our email inbox by FormSubmit, and our inbox is hosted by Microsoft. These pages are hosted by GitHub, the lettering comes from Google Fonts and the anonymous visit counts are kept by GoatCounter. We don't sell your details or add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't go anywhere are deleted after 12 months. Club and invoice records are kept for 6 years after a club leaves, as tax rules require.

Visitor counts

To see which pages are useful and which posts bring people here, these pages (the home page, the demo site, our guides, this page and the data processing agreement) use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It sets no cookies, saves nothing on your device and doesn't follow you to other sites. It doesn't keep your IP address: it uses it briefly, scrambled, to avoid counting the same visit twice and to work out your country. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. GoatCounter keeps the counts for us and doesn't share or sell them. Team and club sites don't use it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and has no adverts.

Team sites

On a team's site, the club is the data controller and we handle its information for it, under our data processing agreement. Children are shown by first name only, team sites are kept out of search engines, and only the team's coaches can change anything. Parents can sign in with a family code from the coach, which links their phone to their child without storing their name or email.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your details, or ask us to correct or delete them, by emailing hello@touchlinetales.club. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

What's included

  • Matchday. Live match mode for the coach, phone alerts for parents, live score, pitch view and commentary.
  • Stats and fair playing time. A page for every player with appearances, goals and assists, and playing time at a glance so every child gets their fair share.
  • Results and reports. Every game kept, with the coach's match report and an honours board.
  • Matchday graphics. Full time, squad, player of the match and milestones, ready for Facebook and Instagram.
  • The sticker book. A page per child at the end of the season, ready to print.
  • Organising. Availability, squad picking, calendar sync and your sponsors on the site.
  • Improvements, free. Ask for a change from inside the site and it comes straight to us. Small fixes are usually done the same day, and new features go on every team's site.

Safe for children, whatever you pay

Every team gets the same safety settings: first names only, the site kept out of Google, only your coaches can edit, and your club decides whether scores are shown, so you can follow the FA's rule for Under 7s to Under 11s. Your welfare officer can read our checklist and the data processing agreement before anything goes live.

Not included

Subs or payments, and club membership and registrations. Many teams use Touchline Tales alongside the app they use for those.

Questions about paying

When do we start paying?

Not until your site is live. Setup is free, and we'll send you a simple way to pay monthly once your team is up and running.

When you sign up. You pay £5 a month by card or Direct Debit on Stripe's secure page, and we set up your site within a few days. Setup is free, and you can cancel any time.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

No. It's month to month. If it isn't working for you, cancel and you won't pay another month. You can ask for a copy of your season, and we delete the site and its data within 30 days.

What does "free improvements" mean?

If there's something you'd like the site to do, tell us. We'll build it at no extra cost, and it goes on every team's site, so everyone gets better together.

Do clubs get a discount?

It's already £5 a month per team, with free setup for every one. Running lots of teams? Get in touch and we'll talk.

What's included

  • Matchday. Live match mode for the coach, phone alerts for parents, live score, pitch view and commentary.
  • Stats and fair playing time. A page for every player with appearances, goals and assists, and playing time at a glance so every child gets their fair share.
  • Results and reports. Every game kept, with the coach's match report and an honours board.
  • Matchday graphics. Full time, squad, player of the match and milestones, ready for Facebook and Instagram.
  • The sticker book. A page per child at the end of the season, ready to print.
  • Organising. Availability, squad picking, calendar sync and your sponsors on the site.
  • Help. Fixes and requests from inside the site come straight to us, and small ones are usually done the same day.

Safe for children, whatever you pay

Every team gets the same safety settings: first names only, the site kept out of Google, only your coaches can edit, and your club decides whether scores are shown, so you can follow the FA's rule for Under 7s to Under 11s. Your welfare officer can read our checklist and the data processing agreement before anything goes live.

Not included

Subs or payments, club membership and registrations, or custom features built for one team. Many teams use Touchline Tales alongside the app they use for those.

Questions about paying

When do we pay?

We send an invoice once your site is live. Nothing is taken automatically.

What happens when the founding season ends?

Before June 2027 we'll agree next season's terms with each founding team. Nothing is charged automatically. If you decide not to carry on, you can ask for a copy of your season and we delete the site and its data within 30 days.

Does the 10% club discount apply to setup?

The 10% is off the season price, from your club's third team. If you're bringing several teams at once, ask us about setup.

Is there a monthly option?

Not at the moment. It's one payment a season, which works out at about £5 a month.

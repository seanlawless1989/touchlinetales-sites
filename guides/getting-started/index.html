Getting started on your team's site

A short video and a guide you can print, for parents and for coaches. They use a made-up demo team, so everything you see works the same on your own team's site.

For parents

Sign in once with your family code, put the site on your Home Screen and turn on match alerts. It takes about two minutes, and there's no app to download or account to make.

  1. Open the link the coach sent, and tap Parents.
  2. Type your family code and tap Sign in. The coach sends each family its own code privately, and both parents can use it.
  3. Add it to your Home Screen: Safari's Share button on iPhone, or Chrome's ⋮ menu on Android.
  4. Turn on match alerts: open it from your Home Screen, tap Match alerts and allow them.

For coaches

Sign in with your email, no password needed, then do Team setup once. It takes about 20 minutes and your site goes live for parents when you finish.

  1. Tap Coach sign in at the top of your team's site and type your email.
  2. Type the 6-digit code from the email, or tap its link. Not there in a minute? Check junk or spam.
  3. Open Team setup: your team, your look, the squad (first names only), fixtures and other coaches.
  4. Sign off with your welfare officer and save. Then send each family its code from the Squad page.

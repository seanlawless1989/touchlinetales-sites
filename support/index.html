Help with Touchline Tales

Quick answers for parents and coaches, and how to reach us.

Getting started in the app

  1. Open the app and type the team code your coach or club gave you. You can paste the link to your team's site instead.
  2. Your team opens. Tap 🔔 Match alerts to get kick-off, goals and full time on your phone.
  3. If the coaches gave you a family code, use it on the team's Parents page to answer availability for your child.

Got more than one child in different teams? Tap Switch team at the bottom of any team page, then Add another team.

Common questions

I don't have a team code

Ask your team's coach or the club. Teams aren't listed publicly, to keep children's details private, so you need the code or the link from them. Type DEMO to look around a made-up team first.

I'm not getting match alerts

Open your team, tap 🔔 Match alerts and check they're on. Then check notifications are allowed for Touchline Tales in your phone's Settings. Tap Send a test alert to try it.

I'm a coach. How do I sign in?

Tap Coach sign in at the bottom of your team's page and enter the email your club gave us. We email you a code: type it into the app. Coaches are added by the team's other coaches or the club.

Can I see the scores?

Each club chooses. For younger age groups the FA asks clubs not to publish scores, so some teams only show them to signed-in coaches.

How do I get my details removed?

See delete my data.

Contact us

Email hello@touchlinetales.club. We usually reply within a day. For anything about your child's team (who's playing, kick-off times), ask the coaches first.

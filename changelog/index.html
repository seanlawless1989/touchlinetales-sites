Changelog

What we've shipped

Every new feature and fix that reaches team sites, newest first. Every team gets every change.

Written by Murph Hartley, founder · Last updated 25 October 2026

  1. 25 October 2026

    A clearer home page

    You can now see the results page, a player page, the sticker book and the coach's matchday screen on the home page, without opening the demo. The demo itself opens with one click, no form first.

    The sign-up form only needs your name, email and club now. Everything else can wait for the call. And there's a new guide for welfare officers and club secretaries, plus this page.

  2. 24 October 2026

    Supporter cheers and a full-screen tactics board

    Anyone with the team's link can send the team a ready-made cheer, with their first name if they like. Cheers are deleted after 30 days.

    The coaches' tactics board now goes full screen, so you can show the team on an iPad at the side of the pitch. In live match mode you can also credit a goal to anyone and add a player who wasn't in the squad.

  3. 23 October 2026

    An alert when the match preview is up

    When a coach saves a preview for the next game, parents get a phone alert.

  4. 22 October 2026

    Rugby union and rugby league

    Team sites now come in rugby union and rugby league versions, with tries, conversions, penalty goals and drop goals, line-ups by shirt number and a sin bin timer. Coaches also get a tactics board, only they can see, for planning moves with players, the opposition and arrows.

  5. 21 October 2026

    Parent sign-in, a privacy page and the demo site

    Each family gets a code, so parents can say whether their own child is available and vote once per family. No parent names or emails are kept.

    Every team site now has a Privacy page for parents. The Photos page is switched off until parents have their own sign-in. Coaches get a sub planner in live match mode, with season minutes and a nudge when someone is due more time. And anyone can try the made-up Sample Town demo site.

  6. 19 October 2026

    Touchline Tales opens to other teams

    The home page went live, with a Team setup page so a new team can be set up quickly. Fixtures can be brought in from FA Full-Time, and each fixture can carry the meet time, kit, pitch, directions and a postponed alert.

    Also this week: parents choose which alerts they get, coaches see who hasn't answered availability and can send a WhatsApp reminder, and there are goal of the month votes, match previews and a season story for every player.

  7. Before launch

    Where it started

    Live match mode with phone alerts, a page for every player, results and match reports, matchday graphics, the sticker book, calendars and the Help and changes page for coaches. Team sites were kept out of search engines from the start, and each club chooses whether scores are shown.

Got something you'd like changed? Coaches can ask from the Help and changes page inside their team site, or email hello@touchlinetales.club.

Your data

Delete my data

How to remove your account, your phone's alerts or your child's details.

Parents

Parents don't have an account or give us a name or email. What's linked to your phone, and how to remove it:

  • Match alerts: open your team, tap 🔔 Match alerts, then Turn off. That deletes your phone's alert address straight away. Deleting the app does the same the next time an alert is sent.
  • Family code sign-in: on your team's Parents page, tap Sign this phone out. Your phone's link to your child is removed.
  • Your child's details (first name, match stats, photos): the club is in charge of these. Ask the team's coaches to remove them, or email us and we'll pass it on to the club.

Coaches

Your sign-in account was set up for your club. To delete it and everything that names you (your email, name, role and photo), email hello@touchlinetales.club from the address you sign in with and tell us which team. We delete it within 30 days, usually much sooner, and confirm by email. The team's match records stay with the club, because they belong to the team, not to you.

What we keep

Nothing. Once deleted, it's gone from the team's database. Backups are overwritten within 7 days.

Questions

Email hello@touchlinetales.club. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

What is the Stourbridge and District Youth Football League and which age groups does it cover?

The Stourbridge and District Youth Football League is a Sunday youth football league serving clubs around Stourbridge and the surrounding Black Country area. It sits within FA grassroots football, meaning it follows FA rules on youth football, including safeguarding requirements that touch every volunteer coach and welfare officer. Like most district leagues, it publishes its fixtures and results online through the FA's Full-Time system, which is where most coaches check their Sunday fixture each week (FA Full-Time).

Which divisions and age groups run on a Sunday

The league runs age groups from the youngest mini-soccer formats through to open age youth football, with divisions split by age band and, where numbers allow, by ability. For the younger groups (Under 7s to Under 11s) that means small-sided formats with an emphasis on fair playing time and development rather than published league tables.

Which clubs from the Stourbridge area play in it

Clubs from Stourbridge and neighbouring towns enter teams across the age groups, often several teams per club. If you manage one team within a club, your fixtures, your line-ups and your player of the match awards all live inside that club structure. How do you find the league's latest fixtures and results?

The Stourbridge and District Youth Football League publishes its fixtures and results through the FA's Full-Time system, and clubs pass them on through their own websites and social channels. Knowing where each layer lives saves you a Sunday-night scramble.

Using the FA's Full-Time system to look up a division

Full-Time is where the league's official record sits. Search for the league on the FA's Full-Time site, open the division your team plays in, and you get the season's fixtures, kick-off times, venues and results as the league enters them. It is worth bookmarking your division's page directly rather than clicking through the league's front page every week, because the division URL never changes once you have it. If you manage the team, check your own fixture listing against what your opponents' secretary is telling the group chat, since discrepancies usually mean someone is working from an old version.

Why a fixture can change after it is published

A published fixture is a plan, not a promise. Pitch closures after rain, cup commitments, and rearranged kick-off times all mean a fixture can move after it appears in Full-Time. The league updates the system, but the news often travels faster through club channels, so follow your club's social accounts as well. Once the final whistle goes, getting the score back to parents quickly is its own job, and our guide on sharing results with parents walks through it.

How does the league compare with other district youth leagues like Colchester or Horsham?

Most district youth leagues in England run on the same bones: divisions by age group, weekend fixtures, and results published through The FA's Full-Time system. Stourbridge and District follows that pattern, so its live fixtures and results sit on Full-Time alongside leagues from Colchester to Horsham. The differences are in the details, not the structure.

What this means for a parent moving between areas

If you relocate, you already know how to find fixtures: search the league name plus Full-Time, pick your child's age group, and bookmark the division. The habits transfer. What doesn't transfer is your team's own record of the season, which is why many managers keep a team site of their own, and why our welfare officer checklist covers record-keeping duties alongside safeguarding basics.

How can a club register or join the league?

Entry runs through the annual cycle every grassroots club already knows: county FA affiliation first, then league paperwork.

What the league asks for at the annual general meeting

Clubs confirm their teams for the coming season at the AGM, typically held in early summer before the September kick-off. Expect to bring your county FA affiliation number, team entries by age group from Under 7s upwards, proposed divisions, a nominated club contact, and the league's entry fees. Dates and deadlines are confirmed at the meeting itself, so someone from the club needs to attend or send apologies and paperwork in advance.

Who in a club handles registration

In most small clubs the secretary does it, sometimes with the treasurer chasing fees and the welfare officer supplying safeguarding details for coaches. If you are a parent-coach running one team, ask your club secretary which forms they submit on your behalf and which fall to you.

How should parents follow their child's team's fixtures each week?

Most leagues, including the Stourbridge and District Youth Football League, publish fixtures through club secretaries, who pass kick-off times and venues down to managers, who then pass them to parents. Under FA youth football formats, match lengths and pitch sizes vary by age group, so always check what your child's age band actually plays before planning your Sunday.

The Saturday-night WhatsApp problem

The group chat is where matchday information lives and dies. Kick-off times get buried under forty messages about car sharing, a postponement arrives while you're at work, and nobody is sure which parent is bringing the bibs. What tools help grassroots leagues publish FA grassroots youth football fixtures?

Most district leagues, including ones like Colchester and District Youth Football League, publish fixtures through the FA's Full-Time system, which generates a public league site with divisions, fixtures and results. That solves the league's job: one authoritative list every week.

What Full-Time does and does not do for a single team

Full-Time is built for the league, not for your Under 9s. It shows the fixture list and scores, but it does not replace the group chat, keep a season-by-season record per child, or carry your club crest and colours. The retyping of line-ups on Sunday night stays with you.

Who runs the league and how do you contact them?

A district youth league like this one is run by a small committee of volunteers: a league secretary who handles general correspondence and club matters, a fixtures secretary who owns the fixture list and rearrangements, and a welfare officer who is the point of contact for safeguarding. Many leagues also appoint a treasurer and a discipline or registrations officer. You can usually find the current officers' names and email addresses on the league's Full-Time public page or its own website.

Which officer to contact for which problem

Send fixture queries (postponements, kick-off changes, pitch issues) to the fixtures secretary, ideally before the deadline set in league rules rather than on the morning of the match. Discipline matters follow the FA route: report concerns through your club secretary, who deals with the county FA, not the league directly. Anything involving a child's welfare goes straight to the league welfare officer, copied to your own club welfare officer. For everything else, the league secretary is the safe first stop.

FAQ

What day do Stourbridge and District Youth Football League matches kick off?

Matches are played on Sundays, as with most district youth leagues. Exact kick-off times vary by age group and venue and are published each week through the FA's Full-Time system, then passed down by club secretaries to managers and parents. Confirm with your manager by Friday rather than waiting for Saturday.

Is the Stourbridge district youth football league the same as the Black Country league?

No. The Stourbridge and District Youth Football League serves clubs around Stourbridge and neighbouring Black Country towns, but it is its own league with its own committee, divisions and entry process. If you are unsure which league your club's teams play in, ask your club secretary or check the league's Full-Time page.

How do I find Hull and district youth football league fixtures if we move area?

The habit transfers: search the league name plus Full-Time, open your child's age group, and bookmark the division page, since that URL stays stable all season. District leagues share the same structure, so the fixture page will look familiar even though the division names and clubs differ from Stourbridge.

Can I look up results for an Under 7s team, or are they non-competitive?

For the youngest groups, Under 7s through Under 11s, the league plays small-sided formats with an emphasis on development and fair playing time rather than published league tables. Older age groups have results entered into Full-Time as normal. Check your division's page on Full-Time to see what is recorded for your age band.

Who do I contact if a fixture is postponed at short notice?

Fixture queries, including postponements and kick-off changes, go to the league's fixtures secretary, ideally before the deadline in league rules rather than on match morning. Your own club secretary or manager should pass the news on to parents, so check the club's usual announcement channel first if the weather turns.

About

Who's behind Touchline Tales

A team website made by a grassroots coach, for the coaches, parents and welfare officers who keep junior sport going.

MH

Murph Hartley, founder

I coach a junior football team. I built our team's website so parents who couldn't make it on a Sunday could still follow the game, and so every child's season was kept somewhere better than the group chat. Touchline Tales is that site, made for your team.

Follow what we're building on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Why it exists

On matchday, the coach is the one with the line-up, the subs and the score, and also the one meant to be typing it all into WhatsApp. The goals, the first games and the best seasons end up buried under forty messages about kit and lifts. A team site that the coach updates in a few taps from the touchline fixes that, and keeps the whole season, child by child.

How it's run

  • Small on purpose. Touchline Tales is a small UK business. When a coach sends a fix or a request from inside their site, it comes to me, and small fixes are usually done the same day. You can see what's changed on our changelog.
  • Children first. First names only, team sites kept out of search engines, and only the team's coaches can change anything. Your club decides whether scores are shown.
  • Your club stays in charge of its data. The club is the data controller and we process the data for it, under a data processing agreement the club accepts before anything goes live. Each team's database is kept separately, in London.
  • No real children in our examples. Every picture on this site is from our made-up Sample Town teams.

Get in touch

Questions from welfare officers, club secretaries and coaches come straight to me at hello@touchlinetales.club. If you'd like to see it working first, open the demo site, or read our checklist for welfare officers.

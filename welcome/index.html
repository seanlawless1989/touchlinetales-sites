What happens next

  1. Check your email. Stripe, who handle our payments, sends your receipt. If you chose Direct Debit, the first payment comes out in a few working days.
  2. We'll be in touch within a working day to ask for your crest, colours, fixtures and last season's results, and to book a quick 15-minute call if you'd like one.
  3. Your site goes live within a few days. We'll send you the link, a coach's guide and a ready-made message for your parents' group.

Please don't email us any children's details yet. We'll ask for first names once your welfare officer has signed off, and our welfare officer's checklist and data processing agreement are ready for them.

Changing or cancelling

It's month to month with no notice period. To cancel, change your card or ask anything, reply to your receipt or email hello@touchlinetales.club.

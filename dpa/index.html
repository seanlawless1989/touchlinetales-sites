Data processing agreement

How we handle your club's information when we run your team's site. Your club is in charge of it; we look after it for you.

Last updated October 2026

This agreement covers how Touchline Tales handles a club's information when it runs a team site. The club accepts it on the site's Team setup page, where the secretary or chair ticks the box and their name, role and the date are recorded.

1. Who this is between

The club named on the Team setup page ("the club") and Touchline Tales, a sole-trader business ("we"). The owner's full details are available to the club on request.

2. Roles

The club is the data controller. We are the club's data processor. We only use the club's personal data to provide the team site, and only on the club's instructions: this agreement, the club's choices on Team setup, and what its coaches ask for through the site. If we think an instruction breaks data protection law, we'll say so.

3. What the processing is

  • Purpose: running the team's website: fixtures, results, stats, match alerts, availability, player profile pictures, graphics and the sticker book.
  • Whose data: players (children), the coaches, parents who sign in with a family code, and visitors who turn on alerts, vote or send a cheer.
  • What data: players' first names, shirt numbers, match and training records, awards, availability, coach notes and reports, and player profile pictures the club has parents' consent for; coaches' email addresses, sign-in records and change requests; each player's family code, and for each parent's phone that signs in with it a random sign-in id, which player it's linked to and when (no parent names or email addresses); alert addresses and vote codes from visitors' phones; supporter cheers, with the sender's first name if they give one and a random code from their phone (deleted after 30 days).
  • Never: surnames, dates of birth, ages, schools, addresses or health information.
  • How long: while the club uses the site, then 30 days (section 9).

4. Confidentiality

Only the owner of Touchline Tales has access to the club's data. Anyone we bring in later will be bound by confidentiality.

5. Security

Each team has its own separate database in London. Coaches sign in with an email link, and the database only lets signed-in coaches change anything. Parents sign in with a family code, which only lets them answer availability for their own child and vote; wrong codes are limited to five an hour per phone, and a coach can replace a code at any time. Private records (availability, attendance, family codes, parent sign-ins, change requests and alert addresses) are locked so visitors can't read them. Team sites are kept out of search engines, and connections are encrypted.

6. Sub-processors

The club agrees to us using Supabase (database and sign-in, with data stored in London), GitHub (web hosting), Google Fonts and jsDelivr (lettering and code), the Apple, Google and Mozilla push services (match alerts), and Anthropic (Claude, which helps us handle coaches' change requests). We'll email the club at least 30 days before adding or changing one, and the club can object and leave. Each is bound by data protection terms at least as strong as these. Transfers outside the UK rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK's approved contract clauses.

7. Helping the club

We'll help the club answer requests from parents to see, correct or delete information within 7 days of being asked. If a request comes to us, we'll pass it to the club within 2 working days. We'll help with any data protection impact assessment the club needs.

8. Breaches

If we find a breach affecting the club's data, we'll tell the club's welfare officer and secretary without undue delay and within 48 hours, with what we know and what we're doing about it. Reporting to the Information Commissioner's Office, where needed, is the club's decision, and we'll give the club what it needs to do it.

9. Ending

When the club stops using Touchline Tales, it can ask for a copy of its data (a spreadsheet or database export) within 30 days. We then delete the database, the site and any copies within 30 days and confirm by email. The club can also ask us to move its database into an account the club owns.

10. Checks

We'll give the club the information it reasonably needs to show we're keeping to this agreement, and answer its questions in writing.

11. Law and contact

This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales. Questions to hello@touchlinetales.club. Parents can read how their child's details are handled on the "Privacy" page of their team's site.
